## Outreach Execution Best Practices & Conversion Tips

1. **Email Personalization**: Replace bracketed fields (`[Name]`, `[Editor]`) with the specific author or editor's first name found on their byline or "About / Contact" page.
2. **Timing**: Send pitches on Tuesday through Thursday mornings (9:00 AM – 10:30 AM recipient local time) for highest open rates.
3. **Value Proposition First**: Never ask for a link without highlighting how it improves their reader's experience (e.g. mobile responsiveness, ad-free speed, eliminating PDF downloads).
4. **Follow-up Protocol**: If no response after 5 business days, send a gentle 1-sentence follow-up:
   > *"Hi [Name], just following up on this quick tool suggestion for your [Article Title] guide—no worries at all if your editorial calendar is already full!"*
